# Grade 8A Grammar Worksheet — Unit 1

ورقة عمل تفاعلية في قواعد اللغة الإنجليزية للصف الثامن أ، للمعلمة جميلة بدرية.

An interactive English grammar worksheet for Grade 8A (Teacher: Jamila Badriyeh).

## Contents
- **A** – Word forms (direct / directions, communicate / communication, collect / collection, locate / location)
- **B, C** – Present Simple: positive and negative
- **D** – Present Simple questions (do / does)
- **E** – Present Progressive, including future plans
- **F** – Present Simple vs. Present Progressive (stative verbs)
- **G** – Free writing: "At the beach"

## How students use it
1. Write your name at the top.
2. Answer each part and press **Check**: correct answers turn green and wrong ones turn red.
3. When you finish, press **Copy my results** and paste them in Google Classroom for your teacher.

Works in any browser, including Chromebooks. It is one HTML file and needs no installation.
